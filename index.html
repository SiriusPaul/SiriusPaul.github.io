About Me

Hello! I'm Sirius Paul, a final-year undergraduate student passionate about machine learning and natural language processing.

I am currently applying for PhD programs starting Fall 2026. My research explores how language models can reason about complex tasks, with a focus on interpretability and robustness.

I have had the privilege of publishing at top-tier conferences including NeurIPS, ICML, and ACL. I'm looking for labs that value rigorous methodology and impactful research.

Research Interests

Large Language Models

Understanding and improving the reasoning capabilities of LLMs through mechanistic interpretability and structured prompting.

Natural Language Understanding

Building models that achieve deeper semantic understanding, particularly in few-shot and zero-shot scenarios.

AI Safety & Alignment

Ensuring AI systems behave reliably and align with human intentions, focusing on eval frameworks and red-teaming methods.
